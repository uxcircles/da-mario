Our story

Thirty years under the portrait wall.

Da Mario is a small, family-owned Italian on Endell Street. It has been feeding Covent Garden for more than thirty years: theatre-goers, office lunches, birthdays and regulars who have been coming for decades.

The walls are lined with black-and-white portraits of actors with Italian roots. The tables are dressed in white linen. In the kitchen it's fresh pasta, veal, fish and seafood, and home-made desserts, including Maria's apple tart. In summer, tables go out on the pavement.
